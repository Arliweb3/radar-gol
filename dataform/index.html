How to protect yourself
  • Use a VPN to hide your real IP and location.
  • Keep your browser and operating system updated.
  • Review and revoke site permissions (location, camera, mic) regularly.
  • Use a privacy-focused browser or tracker-blocking extensions.
  • Never grant location, camera, or microphone access to sites you don't trust.
